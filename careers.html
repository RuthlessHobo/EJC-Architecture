Why EJC

A small studio where your work is visible — on the drawings and on site.

At EJC Architecture, the person who draws a detail sees it built. We work across contemporary residential projects — new builds, estates, additions and alterations — and we keep the team small so every member carries real responsibility from early on.

That means proper technical production: clean Revit models, resolved details, disciplined file management, and documentation that goes through council the first time. It also means fast feedback and room to grow your craft on whole projects, not fragments of them.

The role

Who we're looking for.

Based in Pretoria, you'll work primarily in Revit, producing accurate technical documentation and comprehensive council submission packs. You'll work closely with the Principal and Senior Architectural Technologist, contributing directly to the development and delivery of projects from design through to submission.

Apply now

Required qualifications & experience

  • A degree or diploma in Architecture or Architectural Technology
  • Minimum 1–2 years' experience
  • Proficient in Revit
  • Technical background
  • Council submission experience

Essential skills

  • Good knowledge of municipal council submission requirements and processes
  • Strong problem-solving and design-thinking abilities
  • Ability to accurately interpret and implement instructions
  • Clear and professional communicator with a collaborative, proactive approach
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment
  • Ability to manage confidential information and perform effectively under pressure
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy in technical documentation

Behavioural attributes

  • Flexible, resourceful, and self-motivated
  • Displays initiative and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Excellent judgement and high-level professionalism
  • Enthusiastic about personal and professional development
  • Willingness to work additional hours as required, including occasional weekends
