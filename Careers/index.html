At EJC Architecture, the person who draws a detail sees it built. We work across contemporary residential projects — new builds, estates, additions and alterations — and we keep our team small so that every member carries real responsibility from early on.

That means proper technical production: clean Revit models, resolved details, disciplined file management, and documentation that goes through council the first time. It also means fast feedback, direct access to the work that matters, and room to grow your craft on real projects rather than fragments of them.

If that sounds like the studio you've been looking for, we'd like to meet you.

Draftsperson / Technologist

Join our production team and help carry residential projects from design development through council submission and construction documentation.

Pretoria Full-time 1–2 yrs experience Revit

The role

Who we're looking for

Based in Pretoria. You'll work in Revit daily, producing accurate technical documentation and council submission packs alongside the design team.

Apply now

Required qualifications & experience

  • A degree or diploma in Architecture or Architectural Technology
  • Minimum 1–2 years' experience
  • Proficient in Revit
  • Technical background
  • Council submission experience

Essential skills

  • Understanding of the architectural production process and file management
  • Strong problem-solving and design-thinking capabilities
  • Ability to accurately interpret instructions and present design decisions
  • Clear communicator with a collaborative, proactive attitude
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment
  • Able to manage confidential information and work under pressure

Behavioural attributes

  • Flexible, resourceful, and self-motivated
  • Displays initiative and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Excellent judgement and high-level professionalism
  • Enthusiastic about personal and professional development
  • Willingness to work additional hours as required, including occasional weekends
